No fandom has been left at the altar more times than this one. The 200X line died in 2004 with the story half-told. Mattycollector ran the Classics line on a subscription model that crashed its own servers every sale day and trained an entire fandom to panic-buy. Super7 inherited the keys and immediately asked for $600 to crowdfund a Snake Mountain. Then Origins, the line that was supposed to bring He-Man back to a Walmart peg, got quietly exiled to Mattel Creations and online ordering. If you collect MOTU, your default emotional register is cautious hope braced for the next cancellation. The FigurePinner database tracks 1,497 of these figures across 526 characters and ten distinct lines -- and the value story is completely different depending on which line you are holding. 
He-Man (84 releases) and Skeletor (77) dominate the character count the way Batman dominates DC Multiverse -- which means most He-Man and Skeletor figures are common and sell at or near retail. The money is in the supporting cast, the dead lines, and the vintage cards. Start with the line, not the character.
