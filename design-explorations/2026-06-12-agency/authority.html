02 · The data

Prices with receipts

Nothing on a FigurePinner card is a guess. Every price is computed from real eBay sold listings — recorded sales, filed the way a reference desk files anything: with a method you can check.

Median
The middle of the recorded sales for that figure — the number the market actually clears at.
P10–P90 range
The true spread: where the recorded sales land once the outliers on both ends are set aside.
Confidence
Earned from comp depth. More recorded sales behind a figure, more trust in its number.
