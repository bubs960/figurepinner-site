RULE ONE: ONLY SOLD COMPS COUNT

A "comp" is a comparable sale — a recent, completed transaction for the same figure in the same condition. Active listings tell you what sellers want. Sold listings tell you what buyers paid. [...] The median of that range is your honest market value.

  • Loose, complete — the baseline most comps represent.
  • Loose, incomplete — routinely 30–50% of a complete example.
