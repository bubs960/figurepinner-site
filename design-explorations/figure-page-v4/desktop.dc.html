WHY THIS ONE STILL MOVES

The Ragnarok wave had one job: get the Hulk BAF into as many carts as possible. Hela did it better than anyone else in the case. Un-helmeted Blanchett head, halftone face printing back when that was still new for Hasbro, and the arm — the piece everybody needed to finish the Hulk.

That BAF arm is doing real work in the price. Sealed examples carry the arm; loose ones usually don't, and the comps split accordingly. If a loose listing claims "complete," ask about the arm before you pay MOC money.

Street date is the one thing we can't pin exactly — reviews put it on shelves by August 2017, and that's as precise as the record gets. We'd rather say so than make up a date.
